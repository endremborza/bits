Napoleon's problem

Given a circle and its center, divide the circle into four equal arcs using only a compass.

O is the center of the circle and A a point on it. Write moves separated by spaces: capital letters are points, the lower case letter is the move. A circle is its center followed by a point on it (BO) or by two points whose distance is its radius (BOA).

BOcdraw the circle around B through O
BOAcdraw the circle around B with radius |OA|
OApBname B any new point of circle OA
OAxBOCwalk clockwise around OA from the point facing the center of BO; name C the first new point where they cross
ABCDlast word: the four answer points

A new name must be an unused letter, a new point must not already be named, and no circle may be drawn twice.
