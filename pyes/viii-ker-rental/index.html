Rent vs. Size

Strong positive size–rent correlation, but the price/m² colour gradient shows the per-m² rate falls as apartments get larger — tenants pay more in total but get a bulk discount per square metre.

Market by Apartment Size Bracket

Supply, median rent, and median price/m² across five size categories (unique listings).

Small (36–55 m²) units dominate supply. Studios carry the highest price/m² — roughly 15–20% above the medium segment. XL apartments (>100 m²) are relatively scarce; their lower price/m² reflects the bulk-size discount.

Days on Market by Apartment Size

Distribution of listing duration (first to last snapshot) split by size bracket, capped at 180 days. Tracks actual listing IDs.

Studios tend to leave the market fastest — high demand relative to supply. Larger apartments (76–100 m², XL) show fatter tails, indicating a segment that sits unsold longer, likely due to higher absolute rents narrowing the tenant pool.

Listing Duration (Days on Market)

Overall days-on-market distribution and survival curve across all unique listings.

Median days on market is around 28 days — most listings turn over within a month. The survival curve drops steeply in the first two weeks, then flattens, revealing a persistent tail of hard-to-let properties. Listings surviving beyond 90 days represent ~20% of the market and are likely overpriced relative to comparable units.
